Debugging

Same skillset in a different domain.

New sensor ready to go, but...

...but nothing happens, not even an error. Could it be the...

  • Code? the wrong pins, the wrong config, the wrong constructor?
  • Driver? the wrong defaults, similar but different chip, incomplete implementation?
  • Wiring? signals swapped, a loose jumper, a bad breadboard or solder joint?
  • Power? no power to the sensor, wrong voltage, grounds aren't joined?
  • Part? is it dead, fake, or a different version?

 Where do we start?

Stop guessing, start debugging

  1. Look first: inspect for damage, heat, magic smoke, a burnt smell? Check cables
  2. Check power: Measuring voltage & ground, check signal directions and pins.
  3. Make it fail: the smallest test that consistently reproduces the issue.
  4. Change one thing at a time: and write down what you changed.
    "Remember kids, the only difference between screwing around and science is writing it down." Adam Savage, MythBusters
  5. Get a fresh view: explain it, or ask online/ai. Include code, wiring & the error.

Check power & cables

  • Cable: Some USB cable only charge. Use a known‑good data cable.
  • Battery banks: Some switch off when the board draws too little current.
  • Voltage: Ensure the right voltage level is used for your device and any components.
  • Ground: Has to be shared by every board and supply. Breadboard rails can be split.
  • Under load: Resets when a motor or Wi-Fi starts? A brownout. A meter misses it.

Wiring: look, wiggle, swap

Fine sometimes, and flaky others? Maybe something moved.

A good solder joint is smooth and concave. Lumpy or grainy may not connect at all. Headers must be soldered, not just pushed in.

Press on wires and parts as it runs. Comes and goes as you move? A connection. Changes with just a hand nearby? A floating input: add Pin.PULL_UP/DOWN.

Another wire, another sensor, another board. A part you know works tells you which half is broken.

Adafruit: common soldering problems

Good and bad solder joints

  • If you've done the soldering, check your joints carefully
  • Breadboards: the two long rails on each side are separate, and some are split in the middle. Check with a meter.
  • Jumper wires break inside the plastic. If in doubt, swap it.

Make time visible

No debugger on the board? Time your code with a spare pin, or measure with ticks.


dbg = Pin(DEBUG_PIN, Pin.OUT)   # any free pin
dbg.on(); read_sensor(); dbg.off()
      

t0 = time.ticks_us(); read_sensor()
print(time.ticks_diff(time.ticks_us(), t0))

Logic analyser: your pin is high while your code runs, once per interrupt.

MicroPython Debugger

GHI's extension flashes its own MicroPython fork with a debug engine on a second USB serial port. It can't step into native or viper code, and caught exceptions don't stop. debugpy (https://github.com/micropython/micropython-lib/pull/1022) was a previous attempt, still open, not released.

REPL as a makeshift breakpoint

Wait in your main.py with an infinite loop + Ctrl-C: globals stay at the REPL.


# main.py
debug = {}               # global: kept
def main():
    while True:
        reading = sensor.read()  # local: lost
        debug['reading'] = reading  # copy it
        time.sleep(1)
main()                   # Ctrl-C any time
      
KeyboardInterrupt:
>>> reading
NameError: name 'reading' isn't defined
>>> debug
{'reading': 4095}
  • An uncaught exception keeps them too: main.py and the REPL share their globals.
  • You can look, and manually execute code, but not step through.
  • Only for MicroPython, CircuitPython starts a fresh REPL each time.

Why did it reset?

machine.reset_cause() says why the board last started. Ask first thing in main.py.

  • PWRON_RESETpower came on: plugged in, or the supply dipped (a brownout)
  • HARD_RESETthe reset button, machine.reset(), or a crash in C
  • WDT_RESETthe watchdog: your code hung and stopped feeding it
  • DEEPSLEEP_RESETit woke from machine.deepsleep()
  • SOFT_RESETCtrl-D or your editor restarted MicroPython, not the chip

Not every board tells them all apart: what it can't, it reports as one of the others.
